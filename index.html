Responsible Disposal

Why Choose MOCO Junk Bros?

We don't just haul it to the nearest dump — we sort through everything and handle it responsibly so you can feel good about where your junk goes.

Donate

Anything still usable — furniture, clothes, or household items — we bring to local charities and families in need. We donate everything that can be saved before anything else.

Recycle

We recycle as much of your waste as possible — metals, electronics, cardboard, and more. We do our part to keep Montgomery County green and out of the landfill.

Landfill

Whatever truly can't be salvaged ends up at the landfill — and nothing else. You can rest assured knowing your junk was disposed of the right way.
